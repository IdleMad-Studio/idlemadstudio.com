Hearwood Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-23

Hearwood is a Windows app that reads text aloud. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: Hearwood makes speech on your PC. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, the Microsoft Store version makes no network connections, and it does not collect your information.

Speech runs on your PC

Hearwood's voices and speech model come with the app and run on your PC's processor. The text it reads is not sent anywhere. It is not saved to disk or written to logs: text in the Hearwood window is kept only while the app is running.

Reading selected text

When you press the read hotkey (Ctrl+Alt+S unless you change it), Hearwood copies the text you have selected by pressing Ctrl+C in the app you are using, reads it, and then puts back what was on your clipboard before. If nothing is selected, it reads the text already on the clipboard, as does Speak clipboard in its tray menu. Hearwood uses the clipboard only when you ask it to read, and only on your PC.

Information stored on your PC

Hearwood stores its settings on your PC only: the voice, the speed, your hotkeys and whether it starts with Windows. None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else. Uninstalling the Microsoft Store version removes these settings. If you have also used a copy of Hearwood from outside the Store on the same PC, the two share one settings folder, and that folder stays after uninstalling.

No network connections

The Microsoft Store version of Hearwood includes its speech model and makes no network connections. Copies built from source instead download the model once, the first time they run, from Hugging Face at a fixed address, and check it before use. That download sends nothing about you or your text, and Hugging Face's own privacy policy applies to it.

Starting with Windows

Hearwood can start in the notification area when you sign in to Windows. This is off until you turn it on from Hearwood's tray menu, and you can also turn it off in Windows' Startup apps settings.

Microsoft

Microsoft handles installing and updating Hearwood through the Microsoft Store, and Windows may send Microsoft diagnostic information such as crash reports, as set in your Windows privacy settings. Microsoft's privacy statement covers that information. Hearwood adds nothing of its own to it.

What we do not do

We do not collect personal information, show ads, use analytics or tracking tools, sell or share data, or build profiles. Hearwood is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same address with a new effective date.
